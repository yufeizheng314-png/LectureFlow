LectureFlow adapts the non-main-actor AVAudioEngine tap and producer-before-file-close
lifecycle. Its bounded disk queue, PCM WAV store, recovery checkpoint and fixed ASR
window ownership are newly implemented. The original swallowed write/conversion
errors are deliberately replaced with explicit error propagation.
